Our Story

A Sanctuary of
Excellence & Faith

The Pentecost Convention Centre (PCC) stands as one of the most remarkable achievements in West African Christianity — a 250-acre world-class facility built entirely by The Church of Pentecost through its own resources.

Inaugurated in 2013 and commissioned in 2015, PCC was designed to host the Church's General Council Meetings and major assemblies. Today it has become a landmark institution — welcoming religious bodies, government agencies, trade unions, and international organisations.

Located in the serene town of Gomoa Fetteh, just over an hour from Accra's Kotoka International Airport, PCC combines spiritual purpose with world-class infrastructure.
